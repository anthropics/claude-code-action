What to change

Cache is not your problem. At 97.8% hit and 36.6× reads-to-billed, there is nothing to win by restructuring context. Spend optimisation effort on prompt scope instead.
Check the model split. Opus 5 handled 61% of billed tokens. Where that work was mechanical (formatting, file moves, log reading), Haiku 4.5 — already 27% of volume — absorbs it far cheaper.
Four projects, 84% of spend. C--Users-NoOne, the cloud-session dir, claude-code-action, and emberfall-godot dominate. The emberfall-godot figure is 19.4% from a single session — worth knowing what that one was.
